PRO TIPS FOR BEGINNERS

@cesare_shapable

👉Follow for fat loss done right. Most people buy protein based on price or flavor without checking what they’re actually getting. Here’s how to choose properly. There are three main types: -Concentrates contain more caseins, fats, carbs, and lactose. -Hydrolyzed is pre digested, not very satiating, and usually overpriced. -Isolates are pure whey protein with very low lactose, fine even for many people with intolerances. Choose isolates unless you have a specific reason not to. Next, check the ingredients list. Some brands cheat by adding cheap fillers like creatine, glycine, or glutamine to inflate the protein number artificially. Labs measure nitrogen to calculate protein content, and these fillers contain nitrogen without providing complete amino acids. Only buy protein that shows the full amino acid profile with all twenty amino acids listed in grams. No list means skip it. Finally, check leucine. It should be at least ten percent of total protein. If the product has seventy seven grams of protein per serving, leucine should be seven point seven grams or more. Leucine drives muscle protein synthesis. Without enough, you’re not maximizing growth. 👉Follow for fat loss done right.
